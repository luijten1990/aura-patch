## Use

**01 Peel**  
Remove one patch from its backing.

**02 Apply**  
Apply to clean, dry skin on your upper arm or stomach.

**03 Go**  
Wear for up to 12 hours. Remove gently after use.

**Patience is key**  
For the best results with our patches, use them every day. Some people might feel a difference right away, but it usually takes regular, long-term use to see the full benefits. Each patch lasts up to 12 hours.

## Ingredients

## Patch care

Rotate application sites daily. Single use only. Do not reuse. Press very firmly across the patch on the skin for at least 30 seconds to ensure optimal adhesion and skin contact.

## Safety first

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Apply only to healthy, unbroken skin. Discontinue use if irritation or adverse reactions occur. Consult a healthcare professional before use if pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medications, or allergic to any ingredients.

## Storage

**Store with care**  
Store in a cool, dry place away from heat and sunlight. Reseal after use.
