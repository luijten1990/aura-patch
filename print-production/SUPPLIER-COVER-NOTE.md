# Note to the printer

Please produce the Aura Patch stand-up pouch and the 40 mm round stickers from this folder.

1. Read `README.md` first. Mockup, print-ready, and proof files are different things. Please work from print-ready PDFs placed on your dieline, and return your own prepress proof.
2. Pouch canvas on our current flat proof: 130 x 160 mm per panel, 260 x 180 mm spread, 5 mm bleed. Please replace that canvas with your official dieline. The red construction lines on our proof are provisional. We still need seal widths, zipper keep-out, gusset, and tear-notch position from you. The request list is in `02-pouch-design`.
3. Sticker: 40 mm circle, 6 per sheet, 3 mm bleed, CMYK, cream ground. Gold is either foil or metallic ink. Please wait for that choice in `08-print-specifications` rather than picking a process.
4. Do not redraw the barcode. The code on our proof is nominal. We will supply a file from the registered product code, or tell you to leave it off.
5. Do not “correct” ingredient copy. Set the signed declaration only. Our marketing site and the current pouch proof do not match, and that has to be settled before plates.
6. Guide colours (green bleed, blue trim, red construction) must not print.
7. Please return a digital prepress proof and one physical sample. We sign `09-final-print-approval` against that sample before the production run.
