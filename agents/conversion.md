# Conversion Review

Pretend you have never seen this website before.

Answer:

1. What does this company/product do?
2. Who is it for?
3. Why should I care?
4. What should I do next?
5. What makes this trustworthy?
6. What objections might I have?
7. What information is missing?

Inspect:

- hero
- CTA
- navigation
- product/service explanation
- pricing
- trust signals
- testimonials
- FAQ
- forms
- checkout/contact flow (Subscribe & Save vs one-time, cart, checkout)

Identify friction.

Do not invent testimonials, statistics, credentials, reviews, guarantees, or claims.

Recommend improvements based only on information available in the project.
